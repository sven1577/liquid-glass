## 2026-11-08 - Displacement Map Canvas Reuse and LRU Caching in Liquid Glass
**Learning:** In liquid-glass.js, generating SVG displacement maps dynamically creates new HTMLCanvasElements and invokes `canvas.toDataURL()`. On resize events or in apps with multiple glass components, allocating throwaway canvases causes high heap allocation and GC pressure, while repeated base64 image serialization is CPU intensive. By reusing a module-scoped shared canvas, caching generated displacement maps in a bounded LRU cache (keyed by dimensions and parameters), and caching dimensions on element instances, redundant canvas allocations, renders, and DOM writes are eliminated.
**Action:** When working with canvas-derived SVG filter assets or displacement textures, reuse a shared canvas instance across effects and cache serialized map URLs across instances with identical configurations.
