## 2025-08-05 - Dynamic Content Sizing with Liquid Glass Filters
**Learning:** In interactive elements utilizing a complex SVG/canvas glassmorphic filter (such as `liquid-glass.js`), dynamic content updates (like inline form validation states, success/error feedback, or expandable sections) alter the height and layout of the container. If the filter dimensions and offsets are not immediately recalculated, the glass container will suffer from layout-displacement, clipping, or visual alignment issues. Standard CSS transitions alone are insufficient because the canvas/SVG filter coordinates are tightly coupled to the DOM bounding boxes.
**Action:** Always call the sequential recalculation methods (`measure()`, `clampTarget()`, and `refresh()`) on the `LiquidGlass` instances immediately after any DOM updates that mutate element heights or positions, ensuring the glassmorphic backing aligns perfectly with the visual representation.
